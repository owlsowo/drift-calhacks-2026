Real places. Real paper.

The sea and paper photographs in Drift were made by people. No AI-generated imagery is used in this interface.

Moonlit sea and moon detail
Andaman Sea at night, Moonset, Moonlight, photographed in 2009 by Vyacheslav Argenberg. CC BY 4.0. Cropped and resized for the scene; a moon detail is separately cropped from the same photograph. A CSS color wash and small vector lighthouse are overlaid. WebGL gently displaces the water and moonlight below the horizon; the original photograph remains the fallback.
Crumpled paper ball
Paperball.png by Army1987, 2006. CC BY-SA 3.0. The source image is unchanged; browser transforms animate it.
Paper texture
Free crumpled paper texture for layers, 2008, D Sharon Pruitt / Pink Sherbet Photography. CC BY 2.0. Resized and tinted for legibility and mapped onto a continuous, animated paper mesh for the opening.

Interface references

WeChat’s original Drift Bottle and QQ Mail’s bottle interface inspired the sea, bottom dock and opening ritual. Video supplied by the project owner. Their screenshots and video are references, not bundled assets.

The interactive bottle, dock controls and unfolding geometry are web components. The project is not affiliated with Tencent, WeChat, QQ or the photographers.
