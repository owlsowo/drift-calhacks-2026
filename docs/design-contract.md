# Drift interaction contract

Audience: event attendees taking a short creative break. Release writes a beginning; Pick up writes an ending to someone else’s beginning. Both actions finish independently and can retain separate drafts. A beginning can collect many endings from different writers. My bottles keeps the browser’s beginning IDs and completed-pair IDs in first-party cookies and opens all endings for a beginning. Public IDs do not grant editing or hiding permissions.

Direction: the quiet night sea of classic WeChat Drift Bottle. The owner's new screenshot/video brief prioritizes a full-screen coast and very little chrome. The shore has a small wordmark/help control, open water, and three physical dock objects: Release, Pick up, My bottles. No persistent desktop navigation, side instructions, giant idle bottle, or footer. Writing and reading happen on one paper sheet over the same sea. Kalam carries the actual messages in dark ink; small plain system text labels controls. Cool neutral cotton-paper texture and weathered wood sit in the same night light as the sea. Labels explain actions or status; no numbered halves, vision slogans, decorative seals, or redundant instructions. Dates and necessary sample/team provenance remain. The exported image follows the same rule. Tencent screenshots and video are references, not bundled assets. Original generated coast artwork, prompts and provenance are in `docs/coast-art.md`; material and type credits remain in public/credits.html.

Memorable detail: the WeChat reference’s spotlight stages both actions on the water. The desk and header disappear, the coast dims, and a soft cone lands on an oval pool at 50% across and 62% down. On pickup, an assigned bottle fades into that pool; tapping it or pressing Enter opens the letter directly. Back to sea or Escape preserves the claim and any draft. On release, a confirmed beginning flips from the lower-right foreground into the pool, shrinks and fades. Faint elliptical rings replace cartoon splashes. Reduced motion uses brief fades. The legacy catch fallback retains drag and explicit phone-motion support; a home screen never reacts to phone shaking. Two separately written lines sit on a folded sheet. No personality ratings or invented reactions.

Motion: the real locally bundled Noniv WebGPU FFT renderer supplies natural water, moonlight and the blue-hour sky. The fixed camera places the horizon at 40% of the screen; the separate headland overlaps the waterline by five pixels. Help offers pause/resume, reduced motion freezes the scene and hidden tabs stop rendering. Unsupported WebGPU or initialization/device failure reveals the generated background. See `ocean-engine.md` for the adaptation and MIT attribution. The wooden dock keeps the original trapezoid shape, is narrow on desktop, and reaches the bottom edge without a gap.

Viewports: a 360x740 single-screen resting shore, 390x844 phone and desktop. Primary controls >=44px, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, text labels, reduced-motion support. Fonts bundled; no remote font requests. Word-count feedback does not move controls. Drafts persist by identity and round. Never show successful publication before the authoritative backend confirms.

Additional motion viewport: simulated touch phone at 390x844. Synthetic browser motion verifies permission handling and callbacks; it does not verify a physical phone sensor. HTTP LAN previews offer drag/buttons because motion needs a secure context.

Loading: stable composition + named pending action. Empty: draft retained, retry catch. Error: saved draft + recoverable action. Partial: outgoing story may be waiting while completed incoming card remains usable. Permission: hide/report only authorized cards, private tables never client-readable. Success: postcard, export, next round/collection. Each state must be rendered and observed before claiming verification.

Software-only competition evidence: live two-browser demo, permission/race checks, model fixtures/evaluations, source labels and failure recovery. No attendee outcomes or friendship claims without a pilot.
