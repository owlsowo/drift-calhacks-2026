# A reply comes back

A new ending should feel like an invitation to read, without interrupting the words someone is writing. A three-writer local regression reproduced a layout bug: on a 360×800 shore, the normal-flow return notice pushed the dock's bottom to 979px. The saved `return-shore-chrome-before` screenshot and geometry record that failure.

The home notice now sits below the header without moving the full-height shore. In other views it stays inline inside the main content. A new reply does not change views, replace a draft or take focus. Opening the notice selects that exact completed pair; returning to writing restores the independent unfinished beginning. Short landscape prioritizes the notice over the daily caption.

If verification is also required, the shore shows the returned note first. Dismissing or opening it allows the normal writing check to appear in its appropriate view. Writing remains disabled until verification; the server and challenge protections are unchanged. Read-only stories do not require writing verification.

Six synthetic gate-layout checks include return/check priority with writing disabled throughout. These use synthetic local writers, no provider calls, and an inert widget fixture for the gate layout. They do not establish physical-phone, attendee or hosted Claude completion quality.
