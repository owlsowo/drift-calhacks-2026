# Drift gallery captions

Upload the five 1500 × 1000 PNGs in the order below. Each is 3:2 and below Devpost’s 5 MB thumbnail limit. The screenshots and exported card are actual existing product captures, composed into presentation layouts. They were not recaptured as part of this package. Captions identify their environment; the examples are synthetic, not attendee participation.
