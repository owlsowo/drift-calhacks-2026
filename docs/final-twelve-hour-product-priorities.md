## Product implications

1. **Reliable Claude + live state loop.** Shipped in `5e1b6b7`: assignment-bound retries, bounded Hint wait and subscription-based reply confirmation. The intended evidence is loss/replay/navigation tests, not a claim of better model quality. This supports Spacetime's core live-state criterion, Anthropic's consequential use/failure awareness, and a smooth demo.
2. **A keepsake that finishes.** Bound image/font waits during PNG preparation, preserve a usable share/copy route, and test cancellation/denial. Shipped in `b8e6c9a` with controlled Chrome/WebKit fault coverage and an actual PNG download; physical OS sharing remains separate. This is the payoff of the human connection loop; it is more consequential than adding another logo.
3. **Author control of released beginnings.** The original gap was that authors could invite people but could not withdraw their beginning. `author-withdrawal.md` records the implementation and validation scope. An owner-scoped action can reuse existing server withdrawal logic without adding storage. It must protect guests/cookie-only readers, remove associated publications atomically, handle pending checks and explain limits on retracting saved images. This would strengthen a concrete People-First/Claude-fallibility response.

The current rubric makes Meta's human connection, integrated AI, originality and working demo explicit; it does not require a Meta model switch. Anthropic asks for a named narrow community and firsthand/discussed problem; do not invent interviews or founder history. Mana weights meaningful workflow use (30%), usefulness (25%), originality (25%) and execution (20%), plus a usable app bonus; preserve and demonstrate the actual existing kitchen workflow. Figma's recorded real workflow and deliverables remain separate from these product patches. The owner handles submission writing.

The previous winners support these as inferences: GhostWriter's described live insertion favors authoritative visible results, and SnackSnap's described persistent reward favors a successful keepsake. Their awards do not prove causation. The shipped invite, daily shore, unopened markers, coast and paper fix are already adopted and are not counted as new recommendations.
