# Figma stage log

This was an interaction iteration on an existing game. Times are approximate active-work estimates, excluding waits; ratings are the development agent's assessments.

| Stage | Tools | Time | Assessment | Result used in the build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ideation | Existing game, QQ/WeChat references, Figma Make brief | 3 min | 4/5 | Focused on empty-sea recovery and recognizing returned stories. |
| Prototyping | Figma Make | 8 min | 3/5 | Tried shore, empty-pool, writing, returned-story and postcard states. Revised a tall empty note and corrected reversed authorship. |
| Handoff | Figma Make, Codex | 4 min | 4/5 | Selected compact recovery actions, a returned-story seal, dated prompt identity and word-count marks. Kept drafts editable instead of truncating them. |
| Implementation | Codex, React/CSS, Chrome, WebKit | 14 min | 4/5 | Added the selected interactions to the existing app and checked empty-pool recovery, draft persistence and two-writer flows. |

Presentation preparation took about 10 additional minutes. Seven slides were imported into Figma Slides, and an eighth was added with the playable Make prototype. Prototype interactions used sample content; the production game uses SpacetimeDB.
